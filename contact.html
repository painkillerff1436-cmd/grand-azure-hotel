Frequently Asked Questions

Standard check-in is at 2:00 PM and check-out is at 12:00 PM (noon). Early check-in from 10:00 AM and late check-out until 3:00 PM may be arranged based on availability, sometimes with an additional charge. Gold and Platinum loyalty members receive complimentary late check-out as a standard benefit.
Cancellations made more than 48 hours before the check-in date will receive a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours will be charged one night's room rate as a cancellation fee. No-shows will be charged the full reservation amount. Special rates and packages may have specific cancellation terms.
Yes, complimentary valet parking is available for all guests. We have an outdoor parking area accommodating 200+ vehicles, plus a dedicated covered parking for VIP and Penthouse guests. Electric vehicle charging points are available at an additional charge of ₹100/hour.
We welcome well-behaved pets in select rooms on our ground floor. A pet fee of ₹2,500 per night applies. Please inform us in advance and select the "Pet-Friendly Room" option during reservation. Pets are not permitted in restaurants, the spa, or swimming pool areas. We provide pet beds, bowls, and a complimentary pet welcome kit.
Yes. We offer luxury car transfers from Mangalore International Airport (56 km) for ₹3,500 one-way, and helicopter transfers for ₹22,000 per person. We also offer speedboat transfers from Bekal Boat Jetty for a scenic arrival experience. Penthouse guests receive complimentary helicopter transfer as part of their package. Please book at least 24 hours in advance through our concierge.
Yes, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi (up to 500 Mbps) is available in all rooms, suites, restaurants, lobby, poolside, and across the entire resort grounds. Each room can support up to 10 connected devices simultaneously. Business travellers may request a dedicated connection for video conferencing needs.
Yes, private airport transfers and luxury chauffeur services can be arranged anytime. You can select this option while making your reservation or contact our 24/7 concierge desk prior to arrival.
