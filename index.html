About Our Resort

A Sanctuary of Timeless Luxury

Perched on the pristine shores of the Malabar Coast, Grand Azure Hotel & Resort is a landmark of architectural brilliance and unmatched hospitality. Since 1999, we have redefined luxury travel for discerning guests from around the world.

Each of our 150 meticulously designed rooms and suites offers sweeping ocean vistas, bespoke furnishings, and the finest amenities. Our award-winning team ensures every moment of your stay exceeds expectation.

  • ✦ Private beach with dedicated butler service
  • ✦ Award-winning Azure Spa & Wellness Centre
  • ✦ Three signature restaurants & rooftop bar
  • ✦ Helicopter transfers & private yacht charters
